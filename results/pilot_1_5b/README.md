# 1.5B pilot artifacts

This directory is a small, versioned mirror of the end-to-end pilot run on
`WHU140piROOT` with `Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`. It contains data and
provenance only; LoRA checkpoints are intentionally not committed.

## Contents

- `persona_pilot_scenarios.jsonl`: 240 bilingual scenarios (168 train, 36
  validation, 36 test).
- `persona_pilot_candidates.jsonl`: two candidate responses for each of the
  168 training scenarios, plus its generation manifest.
- `persona_pilot_judged_primary.jsonl` and its audit: 12 strict preference
  pairs selected by the Llama-3.1-8B primary judge.
- `persona_pilot_judged_relaxed.jsonl` and its audit: 114 exploratory relaxed
  pairs; this file was not used for the reported Direct-DPO/PC-DPO comparison.
- `persona_pilot_judged.jsonl` and its audit: the dual-judge run. The output is
  empty because the 1.5B secondary judge rejected every pair; the audit is
  retained to make that failure mode inspectable.
- `pilot_eval_test_{base,direct,pc}.jsonl`: 72 deterministic held-out
  predictions per method (36 test scenarios x two prompt variants).
- `pilot_eval_test_all.jsonl`, `pilot_eval_test_scores.jsonl`, and its
  manifest: the combined predictions and 216 structured Llama-8B judge scores.
- `pilot_eval_test_pairwise.csv` and `.md`: paired deltas and bootstrap
  intervals generated by `compare_methods.py`.
- `direct_training_config.json` and `pc_training_config.json`: exact training
  configurations copied from the two remote checkpoints.

The detailed interpretation is in
[`docs/PILOT_1_5B_RESULTS.md`](../../docs/PILOT_1_5B_RESULTS.md). The scores
are exploratory: one seed, 12 strict training pairs, three test families, and
the same 8B judge used for preference mining and evaluation. They establish
pipeline feasibility and a promising direction, not a final paper claim.

The SHA-256 values in the two manifests bind the candidate/evaluation files to
the scenario file. The remote source paths and model identifiers remain in the
training and score manifests.
